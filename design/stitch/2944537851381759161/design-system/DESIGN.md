## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for calm, focused, morning language acquisition. It balances the tactile tangibility of high-grade book paper with modern mobile ergonomics. The visual tone is warm, deliberate, and encouraging, devoid of aggressive gamification banners or high-stress alert motifs.

### Design Philosophy
- **Tactile Paper Quality**: Backgrounds evoke creamy morning vellum (`#FFF9F1`), and foreground surfaces rest on immaculate paper white (`#FFFDF9`) and soft oatmeal tones (`#FBF7F0`).
- **Quiet Academic Temperament**: Sage greens (`#54715A`), warm muted wheat accents (`#E7B75D`), and terracotta corals (`#B95F4B`) create natural focal points without screen fatigue.
- **Typographic Precision**: Vocabulary words stand proud with oversized display geometry, flanked by structured IPA pronunciation, pastel part-of-speech badges, and focused sentence highlights.
- **Organic Soft Geometry**: High continuous radii (20–28px on study cards, fully rounded pills on buttons and status tags) provide a soft, welcoming, and tactile hand feel.

## Colors

The color palette establishes a tranquil dawn study ritual.

### Roles & Rationale
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The base canvas (`#FFF9F1`) eliminates glaring blue-white light. Elevated study cards use `#FFFDF9` to mimic unblemished vellum, with grouped container lists set in `#FBF7F0`.
- **Primary Sage (`#54715A`) & Deep Green (`#3E5442`)**: Conveys calm mastery, steady progress, and positive confirmation. Used for primary CTA buttons, active radio indicators, and mastery tallies.
- **Secondary Wheat Gold (`#E7B75D`)**: Used for review round progress rings, milestone stars, and subtle accomplishment badges. Paired with soft straw backgrounds (`#FAF0DC`).
- **Coral Terracotta (`#B95F4B`)**: Serves as the empathetic error indicator. It signifies needed practice without punishing psychological anxiety. Supported by light coral background pills (`#F7E4DE`).
- **Typographic Brown-Black (`#292621`)**: Softened organic ink black that preserves sharp legibility without harsh synthetic contrast against cream surfaces.
- **Target Word Red (`#D32F2F`)**: Reserved strictly for contextual example sentences to isolate target vocabulary roots and inflections.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Epilogue** for display headlines and main vocabulary targets with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for clear, humanist interface typography, body descriptions, and sentence breakdowns.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Vocabulary Presentation (`display-word`)**: Prominent weight and tight tracking command the user's immediate attention. When the large-font accessibility setting is engaged in the PRD, step up to `display-word-lg`. Long compound phrases dynamically scale to `display-word-mobile`.
- **IPA Phonetics (`phonetic`)**: Standardized with brackets (e.g. `[sə'bɔːdɪnət]`) in `text-secondary` (`#7D756A`).
- **Example Sentences**: Set in `body-md` with relaxed line-height (1.55) to support effortless reading. The targeted term within the sentence must use `highlight-target-word` (`#D32F2F`) in semibold weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The system is optimized for single-handed mobile navigation (standard 390dp viewport baseline). 

### Layout Model
- **Container Structure**: Single-column vertical stack with a locked `screen-edge-padding` of 20dp (`1.25rem`), preserving breathability across card borders.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**:
  - Related atomic elements (word title to phonetic transcription): `0.5rem` (`space-xs`).
  - Content groups inside study cards: `1.25rem` (`space-lg`).
  - Section-to-section separations: `1.75rem` (`space-2xl`).
  - Bottom action dock clearance: Safe area inset + `4.5rem` to avoid thumb collision with navigation bars.
- **Device Constraints**: For screens narrower than 360dp, metric columns stack into balanced 2x2 grids, and batch action buttons convert from full horizontal label rows into compact icon-label pairs.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids dark, stark synthetic dropshadows. Depth is manifested through warm tinted ambient occlusion and subtle tone boundaries that mimic sheets of cream cotton paper resting on a desktop.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Pure `#FFF9F1` background without shadow or outline.
- **Level 1 (Default Sheet & Inset List Cards)**: Background `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid rgba(220, 210, 195, 0.45)`, soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(125, 117, 106, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Learning Focus Card)**: Background `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid rgba(231, 183, 93, 0.25)`, elevated ambient glow: `0 10px 30px rgba(84, 113, 90, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Docks & Dialogs)**: Background `#FFFDF9` with backdrop blur, shadow: `0 12px 36px rgba(41, 38, 33, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is generous, soft, and rounded.

### Shape Scales
- **Study Containers & Flashcards**: `24px` (`1.5rem`) to `28px` (`1.75rem`) smooth squircle corners, creating a welcoming, book-like presence.
- **Action Buttons & Inputs**: Fully rounded pill forms (`9999px` / `button-touch-target`) for primary decision points (e.g. "会 / 不会", "开始晨测").
- **Tags, Badges & Chips**: `12px` to `16px` pill corners to encapsulate part-of-speech identifiers and session status indicators cleanly.

## Components

### 1. Flashcard Word Card
- **Container**: Elevated Level 2 paper surface (`#FFFDF9`), rounded corners at `24px`, internal padding `24px`.
- **Word Header**: Centered `display-word` text (`#292621`), accompanied by the phonetic transcription in `text-secondary` and a circular audio speaker icon button (44×44dp touch area, `#F3EBDD` background).
- **Badge Bar**: Inline horizontal stack of part-of-speech chips:
  - Adjective (`adj`): Background `#E2ECE9`, Text `#3D6B5E`.
  - Noun (`n`): Background `#E1EBF5`, Text `#3A6389`.
  - Verb (`v` / `vt`): Background `#EFE3F2`, Text `#754382`.
- **Content Reveal Area**: Hidden during initial recall in Student Mode; reveals with a clean vertical cross-fade upon tap:
  - English definition and Chinese gloss in `body-md`.
  - Contextual example sentence with the keyword styled in semibold `#D32F2F`.

### 2. Action Controls & Learning Responses
- **Dual Decision Bottom Dock**:
  - "不会" (Incorrect / Missed): Pill button, height `54dp`, background `#F7E4DE`, label `#B95F4B` (`label-lg`), with subtle tactile active state.
  - "会" (Understood / Prompt): Pill button, height `54dp`, background `#54715A`, label `#FFFDF9` (`label-lg`).
- **Answer Verification Phase**:
  - "我错了" (I Was Wrong): Pill button, background `#F7E4DE`, label `#B95F4B`.
  - "我对了" (I Was Right): Pill button, background `#54715A`, label `#FFFDF9`.
  - Optional tertiary link: "会 (移出错词库)" set in sage green with a checkmark outline.

### 3. Review Round Circular Progress Indicator
- Concentric segmented arc or circular gauge:
  - Unfilled track: `#F3EBDD`, thickness `8px`.
  - Completed arc: Gradient or flat stroke of `#E7B75D` (Warm Gold).
  - Center metadata stack: "Round 1" in `label-sm` (`text-muted`), large count "300 / 3637" in `headline-lg` (`#292621`).

### 4. Batch & Category Cards (Library & Wrong Book)
- Rounded container (`20px`), background `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid rgba(220, 210, 195, 0.45)`.
- Left-side folder accent: Subtle warm tab tint (`#DDE8DA` for master lists, `#F7E4DE` for review folders).
- Title in `headline-sm`, subtitle showing word count and date in `body-sm` (`#7D756A`).
- Trailing quick actions: Edit and Delete buttons separated by at least `12dp` to prevent touch collisions.

### 5. Input Fields (Import & Rename)
- Background `#FBF7F0`, border `1.5px solid rgba(125, 117, 106, 0.2)`, rounded `16px`.
- Focused state: Border `#54715A`, outline `3px solid #DDE8DA`.
- Multi-line editor carries line-number guides on the left in `#A8A096` to support the 12-line preview requirements.
